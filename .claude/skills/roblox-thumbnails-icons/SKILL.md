# Roblox thumbnails and icons

## References
- `thumbnails/README.md`: research links for 30 Roblox experiences (with some downloaded images) and `thumbnails/EXTRA-REFERENCES.md`: 30 composition briefs.
- `logos/README.md` and `logos/EXTRA-REFERENCES.md`: 30 icon directions.
- `scripts/fetch_roblox_images.py` fetches public artwork for research into `roblox-image-manifest.json`.

## Rules
- Use other games' art for research only; do not reuse or trace it.
- Icon: 512x512, one bold readable subject, works at small size, no small text.
- Thumbnails: 1920x1080 (16:9), show the core activity or hook, clear focal point, consistent with the in-game style.
- Do not include misleading content, real-money prices, or Roblox branding in ways the Roblox community standards forbid; check current rules on the Creator Hub.
- Describe the composition as a brief (subject, angle, palette, text) when no art tool is available rather than claiming an image exists.
